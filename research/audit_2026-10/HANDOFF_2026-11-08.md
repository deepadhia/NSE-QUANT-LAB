## Question
Does adding an RS55 entry filter (stock 55-session return minus Nifty 500 55-session return, in pp, at decision-date close; NEW entries only, held names exempt per HoldFix) improve `trend_momentum_pit` win rate without hurting the rest?

## Method (VERIFIED)
`research/audit_2026-10/scripts/ab_rs55_filter.py`: subclass of the production `TrendMomentumPITStrategy` (adds `rs55_pct` in `compute_indicators` and filters in `select_entry_candidates`; `rs55_min=None` = production), production engine, config as `minervini_20y.py`, cached universe `results/ab_regime_catchup/universe_cache.pkl` (local, gitignored), 15 slots, post-tax, 2007-01-01..2026-10-30. Missing RS55 blocks the entry (fail safe). Adoption rule fixed before running: win rate AND PF AND CAGR not worse than baseline in FULL and >= 2 of 3 sub-windows, trades >= 60% of baseline.
Arms run: A0 baseline, A1 RS55>0, A4 RS55>=20, A5 RS55>=40. **Not run:** A2 (>=5), A3 (>=10) (user scoped the test). **A5 was added post-hoc** after the baseline bucket table; treat as data-informed.

## Verdict
- RS55>0: no effect (win +0.5 pp is inside the ~±1.8 pp sampling error). Not adopted.
- RS55>=20: win rate +3.9 pp, consistent in all windows, CAGR flat over 20y; but fails the pre-set rule narrowly (full PF 1.83 vs 1.87; 2021-26 CAGR/PF/DD worse). Not adopted; promising but not proven (3rd filter tried, threshold chosen partly after seeing data, no out-of-sample).
- RS55>=40: worse. Not adopted.
- Strategy stays as deployed. Win rate is not a target in itself (AGENTS.md); do not tune thresholds further on this history.

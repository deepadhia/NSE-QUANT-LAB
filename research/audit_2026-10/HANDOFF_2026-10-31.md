## 4. Verdict (honest)
- There is a broad, repeatable pre-cost momentum edge on the Indian small/mid-cap universe 2007-2026 (positive in every parameter combination in DEV, 53/54 in LATE). It is NOT proven as a post-cost alpha: after dated tax and a plausible 40–60 bps/leg for small caps the 20-year edge is ≈ +5–7 pp, but over the last ten years it shrinks to ≈ 0–1 pp (UA) / ≈ 0–2 pp (UB), and 2016-20 was flat vs the index in every EQ-only config. The 2021-26 gain is largely small-cap beta (no-skill basket 21–22%).

## 22. Addendum: lists, stops and open orders (user question 2026-11-01)
**Lists.** (a) The strategy's BUY list is rebuilt from scratch at every month-end close (the decision date) from the whole universe; nothing is carried over except holdings. (b) The Upstox FETCH list (1,108 targets) is NOT refreshed automatically: it is recomputed each run from the bhavcopy panel as of its last date (frozen at 2026-10-30); monthly refresh via Upstox's instrument master is designed, not built (section 19). (c) The OLD server flow's Nifty-500 list is synced semi-annually by the daemon (first trading day of April and October).
**Stops and open orders (as implemented, VERIFIED in `paper/manager.py`).** There are NO resting stop orders (paper, backtest or broker). The stop is a close-based exit: each evening `evaluate_daily_exits` flags close < EMA(100) and writes a SELL order `ORD_EXIT_<date>_<sym>` with status PENDING_OPEN (duplicates are blocked if a PENDING_OPEN/DEFERRED order for the symbol exists); month-end drops (`ORD_DROP_*`) cover holdings that no longer pass the entry filter. Next morning sells run first at the open; a lower-circuit lock sets DEFERRED_LOWER_CIRCUIT and retries each following morning (`deferral_days` counts); buys are skipped on upper-circuit lock or a gap above +2% of the previous close, and any buy orders left PENDING_OPEN after the batch are expired (SKIPPED_EXPIRED); a sell with no execution bar stays pending until a bar exists. Order ids are deterministic (`ORD_BUY_<date>_<sym>`), so re-running a day replaces rather than duplicates. Each evening positions get their `ema100_stop` / `stop_loss_price` / `stop_buffer_pct` refreshed for display. Consequence for real money: an intraday crash is not protected and exits happen at the next open (gap risk is in the backtest too); a broker-side disaster stop would be a deviation from the tested rules and has not been tested.
**Gap found and fixed:** the new full-tier ingestion did not rescale open positions or pending orders when a split was flagged (the old ingestion script did). Now, when a gap is flagged and the adjusted history shows a price factor beyond 5%, `ingest_upstox_fulltier.py` multiplies open paper positions' shares (and pending SELL order shares) by the factor and divides entry prices, last close and order prices by it (value and cost unchanged); demergers are not touched (Upstox does not adjust them, so the factor stays near 1). Tested with fake collections (`test_split_rescales_open_position_and_pending_sell_but_not_other_symbols`); not yet exercised on a real split. Flags are written to `corporate_action_flags` for review.

# Pre-registered experiment 2026-10-31 (written BEFORE any run of the grid below)

Purpose: test whether a rules-based momentum strategy on the cleaned full-market NSE bhavcopy panel beats Nifty 500
(price) after realistic costs AND Indian tax for a small account (Rs 1 lakh start, also Rs 5 lakh), without tuning to a target.

## Honest status of independence (read first)
The user and earlier sessions have already seen full-period (2007-2026 and 2016-2026) results for the committed strategy
and for an "unconstrained" universe (25.47% CAGR claim). Therefore NO period below is a clean out-of-sample.
The split is used to limit *new* selection, nothing more. The final report must say so.

## Data
`data/external/nse_archive/clean_panel.parquet` from `build_clean_panel.py`: raw NSE bhavcopy 2006-01-02 -> 2026-10-30,
renames chained, ETFs/MF/rights entitlements removed, official split/bonus factors applied, extreme residual jumps
(r<=0.25 or >=4 on a clean split factor) adjusted; milder unexplained jumps are LEFT in (disclosed, downward-biased for
held names). Series (EQ/BE/BZ/SM) kept per row so tradability is decided point-in-time.

## Fixed execution model (not tuned)
Signal at month-end close, fills at next session open, +2% gap cap, circuit-lock rules, integer shares, 10% of 50d ADT cap,
statutory fees, DP Rs 14.75/sell, slippage 20 bps/leg base, stress 40/60/100 bps. Tax: `tax_india.py` dated rates, losses carried.
Evaluation 2007-01-01 -> 2026-10-23 (warm-up 2006). Rs 1 lakh start.

## Grid (all runs reported, none dropped)
- Strategy: S0 committed trend_momentum | S1 Clenow (90d exp-regression slope x R^2, close>100DMA, max |1d move| in 90d < 15%,
  exit on close<100DMA) | S2 12-1 momentum (return t-252..t-21, close>200DMA, exit on close<200DMA)
- Tradable universe (point-in-time, from data up to decision date): UA = series EQ, ADT50>=Rs 1 Cr, px>=20 |
  UB = EQ, ADT50>=Rs 25 L, px>=10 | UD = any series incl. SM/BZ, ADT50>=Rs 25 L, px>=10 (replicates the 25% claim's spec)
- Regime overlay on Nifty 500 vs its 200-session SMA: R0 none | R1 no new entries when below | R2 also sell all at next open when below
- Slots: 10 | 15 (hold buffer 2.5x slots). Monthly rebalance.
= 3 x 3 x 3 x 2 = 54 runs.

## Slices (from ONE continuous run each, not independent restarts)
DEV 2007-2015 | VAL 2016-2020 | LATE 2021-2026-10.

## Selection rule (pre-stated)
Finalists = configs with DEV t-stat of monthly excess vs N500 > 0 AND VAL excess CAGR > 0 AND max drawdown no worse than
N500 max drawdown + 15pp over the full run. Among finalists rank by lowest max drawdown after tax at equal-or-better
post-tax CAGR than N500 post-tax. LATE reported for all 54; if fewer than half of the 54 beat N500 in LATE, the verdict is
"edge not stable" regardless of the finalist.

## Success criteria for a RESEARCH_CANDIDATE (pre-tax gross of nothing hidden; post-tax is the headline)
1. Post-tax CAGR (Rs 1 lakh) beats Nifty 500 price CAGR by >= 3 pp over 2007-2026 AND over 2016-2026.
2. Beats the equal-weight no-skill basket of its own universe (gross) over the same window.
3. Still beats N500 at 60 bps/leg slippage.
4. Top-10 trades < 50% of positive P&L; result survives dropping the single best year.
Fail any = not a candidate.

## Known unmodelled items (disclosed up front)
Dividends (both sides), N500 TRI vs price, grandfathering, surcharge, advance-tax timing, impact beyond 10% ADT, T2T/ASM
stage changes after entry, price-band widening, rights/demergers (not adjusted), mild unexplained jumps, ticker reuse.

## Addendum 4 (written before running): Finance-Guru "Champion v1.1 High-TP DNA" on our 20-year full-market data
Source of rules (read from `Finance-Guru/config/settings.py`, `scripts/champion_daily_update.py`, `scripts/signal_generator.py::calculate_rs_n`, `scripts/paper_portfolio_tracker.py`):
- Universe: IPO stocks listed within the last 24 months (ours: first bar of the canonical symbol in the bhavcopy, first bar after 2006-04-01, age <= 720 days; series EQ on the signal date).
- Scan on day T close: >= 50 bars; 20d mean close >= 50, 20d mean volume >= 100,000, 20d mean turnover >= Rs 50 L, close <= 1000.
- Base: 10 bars ending T-1; base_high = max high; depth = (max high - min low)/max high <= 18%; avg_vol_base = mean volume of those bars.
- RS-55 = (stock 55-bar return - Nifty 50 return over the same two dates) x 100; High-TP DNA needs RS-55 >= 40 (v1.0-like baseline: >= 10).
- Breakout: close_T > base_high; vol_surge = vol_T / avg_vol_base in [1.0, 5.0]; extension (close-base_high)/base_high <= 5%.
- Exit: SL = entry x 0.90, TP = entry x 1.25, no trailing, no time exit; SL checked first (gap-down fills at open), SL+TP same bar -> SL.
- One position per symbol at a time.
Fill modes (both reported): AS_CODED = entry at signal-day close (what the repo records; not achievable live); REALISTIC = next open, skipped if open > close x 1.02 or locked up, SL/TP anchored to the actual fill, tiered cost model + statutory fees on both legs, exit locked down -> next session's open.
Controls: (a) random-entry control: for the same year, same IPO-age and liquidity gates but WITHOUT base/RS/volume/breakout conditions, same exits and fills, 2,000 bootstrap draws of the same number of trades; (b) "breakout only" (depth<=18, breakout, vol 1-5x, ext<=5%, RS gate OFF); (c) v1.0-like (RS>=10); (d) High-TP DNA (RS>=40).
Judged on: trade count and trades per year, win rate, mean net return per trade, profit factor, median, share of profit from top 10 trades, p-value vs random control, yearly breakdown, and with the 2021-24 IPO boom removed. No threshold is tuned. Verdict is 'no edge' unless mean net return beats the control at p<0.05 in REALISTIC mode.
Validation of OUR implementation: (i) hand-checkable fixture tests; (ii) comparison with Finance-Guru's recorded Champion signals where the local `ipo_tracker`/paper data overlaps (if present).

## Addendum 6 (written before running): four tests from the strategy search (sources: Raju 52-week-high and low-risk studies via Factor Investor/Quantpedia; NSE Nifty200 Momentum 30 whitepaper; Freefincal liquidity critique)
Same setup as the realistic runs: clean panel, point-in-time EQ universe with history >= 226 sessions, Rs 1 L start, tiered cost x1.0, dated Indian tax ON, next-open fills, fixed before running; none of the parameters below is tuned.
- S3 52WH: eligible UB names with close >= 0.90 x 252-session high of close; rank by close / 252-high (highest first); 15 slots; monthly decisions; hold buffer 2.5x; NO daily trend exit.
- S4 MomLowVol: eligible UB names with close > 200-day MA and 12-1 month return > 0; keep the top 30% by 12-1 return; among them rank by lowest 6M annualised volatility; 15 slots; monthly; buffer 2.5x; no daily exit.
- S5 Momentum30-style: rank by the repo's blended 6M/12M return / 6M volatility score (same construction as NSE's normalised momentum, not its exact z-score); 30 slots equal weight; decisions only at June and December month-ends; buffer 1.0x (hold only the top 30); no daily exit; two universes: UT200 (the 200 most liquid EQ names by 50-day median turnover on the date, Nifty-200 stand-in) and UB (broad). This tests Freefincal's 'liquidity illusion' claim.
- Regime overlay R0 and R1 for S3/S4/S5 (R1 = no new entries when Nifty 500 < 200-session SMA).
- Liquidity split of OUR strategy (S0, R1, 15 slots, no rank-drop exits): UH = EQ with 50-day median turnover >= Rs 10 Cr; UM = Rs 1-10 Cr; UL = Rs 25 L - 1 Cr.
11 runs, all reported. Benchmarks: Nifty 500 price and our S0 UB result (22.2% / 16.6% over 2016-26 post-tax). A candidate is only interesting if it beats S0 UB post-tax over BOTH 2007-26 and 2016-26 or clearly lowers drawdown at similar return. Multiple-testing caveat applies (about 170 configs tried in total this session).
